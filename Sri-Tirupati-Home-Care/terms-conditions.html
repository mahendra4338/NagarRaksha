2. Nature of Services

Sri Tirupati Home Care Services provides trained caregivers, nursing staff, and support personnel for home-based care in Tirupati and surrounding areas. Our services are supportive and caregiving in nature; they are not a substitute for hospital-based emergency medical treatment. In case of a medical emergency, please contact a hospital or emergency medical service immediately.

3. Booking & Scheduling

  • Services can be booked via phone, WhatsApp, email, or our website contact form.
  • Caregiver assignment is subject to availability; we aim to assign a suitable caregiver as quickly as possible, typically within a few hours of confirmed booking.
  • Schedule changes should be communicated to our coordination team with reasonable advance notice.

4. Pricing & Payment

Service pricing depends on the type of care, duration, and frequency required, and will be communicated transparently before service begins. Payment terms will be agreed upon at the time of booking. We do not charge hidden fees beyond what is agreed with the family.

5. Caregiver Conduct & Replacement

All caregivers are background-verified and trained prior to placement. If a family is not satisfied with an assigned caregiver, we will make reasonable efforts to arrange a suitable replacement promptly.

6. Client Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information about the patient's medical condition and care requirements at the time of booking.
  • Ensure a safe working environment for the caregiver within the home.
  • Inform us promptly of any change in the patient's condition that may affect the type of care required.

Service cancellations or rescheduling should be communicated to our team as early as possible. Cancellation terms for ongoing or long-term care arrangements will be discussed and agreed upon at the time of booking.
