Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your family's information.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Sri Tirupati Home Care Services ("we," "our," "us") provides home healthcare services including patient care, elder care, nursing care, supporting care, baby care, and cooking services in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the personal and health-related information of the families, patients, and website visitors we serve.

By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details — name, phone number, email address, and home address, provided when you fill our contact form, call, or WhatsApp us.
  • Care-related information — details about the patient's condition, care requirements, and medical history that you choose to share with us to arrange appropriate caregiving.
  • Service & billing information — records related to the services booked, scheduling, and payments.
  • Website usage data — general, non-identifying technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used only to improve our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Understand your care requirements and match a suitable caregiver or nurse
  • Communicate with you regarding scheduling, service updates, and billing
  • Maintain accurate care records for the safety and continuity of the patient's treatment
  • Respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form, phone, or WhatsApp
  • Improve our services and website experience

We do not use patient health information for marketing purposes without explicit consent.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable administrative and operational precautions to protect personal and health-related information from unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure. Access to sensitive patient information is limited to caregivers, nurses, and coordinators directly involved in a family's care.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only:

  • With the caregiver, nurse, or attendant assigned to your care, as necessary to deliver the service
  • With your treating physician, where care coordination requires it and with your consent
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal request

6. Data Retention

We retain personal and care-related information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain accurate care records, and meet any applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

7. Your Rights

You may request to review, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you or your family member, subject to any care-record retention obligations we may have. To make such a request, please contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies & Website Data

Our website may use minimal, non-invasive technical data (such as browser type) to ensure the site displays correctly. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate the most recent revision.
